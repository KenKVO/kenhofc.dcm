**Abstract (journal).** Scanner and protocol differences limit multi-site AI. This virtual imaging study applied a physics-informed deep network as a unified harmonization model across three lung tasks: structure segmentation, COPD quantification, and lung-nodule quantification. Evaluation before versus after harmonization showed task-dependent gains and demonstrated that a single VIT platform can stress-test harmonization for AI generalizability. *Medical Physics* 52(11):e70084 (November 2025).

**Conference version (SPIE 2025).** *The role of harmonization: a systematic analysis of various task-based scenarios* (Xia, Vancoillie, Sotoudeh-Paima, Zarei, Ho, Tushar, Chen, Dahal, Lafata, Abadi, Lo, Samei). Same three-task VIT design (segmentation, COPD quantification, nodule quantification) with a physics-informed harmonizer. Proc. SPIE 13405, 1340516 (8 April 2025).
