**Abstract.** Deep-learning CT reconstruction kernels can be tuned for pulmonary imaging, but their effect on quantitative COPD biomarkers versus conventional kernels is not fully characterized. This work evaluated quantification accuracy, consistency, and image quality for DL kernels intended for lung CT, in the context of Canon Medical research on PCCT postprocessing / DL reconstruction and GE HealthCare work on novel reconstruction and spectral imaging for lung and bone quantification.

Journal manuscript under review (Ho FC, Zarei M, Bache S, et al.).
