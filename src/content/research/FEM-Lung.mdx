**Abstract.** Parametric response mapping and other lung-function CT biomarkers depend on how the lung deforms between inspiration and expiration. This Virtual Imaging Trials in Medicine contribution modeled patient- and disease-specific respiratory mechanics with finite elements, to simulate personalized lung motion and ventilation heterogeneity for virtual imaging assessment of lung function.
