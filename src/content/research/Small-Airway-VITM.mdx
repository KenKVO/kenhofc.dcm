**Abstract.** Functional small-airway disease is often measured from paired inspiratory–expiratory CT, but scanner physics and reconstruction change the apparent disease burden. This first-author virtual imaging study used patient-informed chest models at both lung volumes to quantify how energy-integrating and photon-counting CT, dose, and reconstruction affect small-airway biomarkers relative to known ground truth.

Proc. Virtual Imaging Trials in Medicine 2024;81. Journal manuscript under review (Ho FC, Kavuri A, Sotoudeh-Paima S, et al.). Related SPIE PRM analysis: [PRM 2024](/research/PRM/).
