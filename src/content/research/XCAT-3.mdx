**Abstract (journal).** Computational phantoms (digital twins) are the patient arm of virtual imaging trials. XCAT 3.0 builds a large library of personalized, anatomically detailed human models from clinical CT using automated segmentation and quality control, spanning diverse age, sex, and body habitus. The library supports scanner-specific simulation, protocol evaluation, and AI testing at population scale. Phantoms may be requested at [cvit.duke.edu/resources](https://cvit.duke.edu/resources/). Code, model weights, and sample CT images are at [xcat-3.github.io](https://xcat-3.github.io/).

Preprint: arXiv:2405.11133 (2024).

**Conference version (VITM 2024).** *Deep learning-driven development of patient-specific digital twins with automatic quality control* (Dahal, Ghojoghnejad, Ghosh, Bhandari, Ho, Kim, Tushar, Abadi, Samei, Lo, Segars). Three modules: (1) deep-learning segmentation of up to ~140 structures from full-body CT, (2) volumetric phantom construction, and (3) automatic QC that flags segmentation failures. On the order of 1,000 patient-specific digital twins of diverse age, sex, and body habitus were released. Proc. Virtual Imaging Trials in Medicine 2024;25.
