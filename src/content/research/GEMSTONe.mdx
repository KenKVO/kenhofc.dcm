**Abstract.** Virtual imaging trials chain patient modeling, scanner simulation, and image analysis across many software systems. Ad hoc handoffs and inconsistent file formats make those pipelines fragile. GEMSTONe separates each module’s computation from a central workflow manager, using declarative configuration, automated parameter checks, standard module interfaces, and centralized path management.

Three example workflows were deployed: a refactor of the [Virtual Lung Screening Trial](./VLST/), a high-throughput CT simulation scheduler, and an interoperable pipeline joining the FDA VICTRE and University of Pennsylvania OpenVCT breast-imaging tools. Engine-level mechanisms alone produced a 3.5× speedup. Refactoring, integrating, validating, and combining VICTRE and OpenVCT took less than five weeks.
