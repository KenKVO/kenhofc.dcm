**Abstract.** Photon-counting CT implementations differ in detector design. This AAPM 2023 virtual imaging trial compared edge-on irradiated silicon-based PCCT with energy-integrating CT for quantitative bronchitis (airway) biomarkers, using computational patients and scanner-specific simulation rather than a single clinical cohort.
