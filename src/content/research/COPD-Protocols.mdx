**Abstract.** COPD (chronic bronchitis and emphysema) needs precise CT quantification, but protocol inconsistency makes biomarkers unreliable. This in-silico study optimized acquisition and reconstruction for cross-sectional and longitudinal COPD CT. Human models at various emphysema and bronchitis stages were informed by COPDGene. A Canon Aquilion ONE Prism model in DukeSim, validated against experimental data, was used to study tube current and kernel sharpness on LAA-950, Pi10, and mean absolute error of emphysema density. Dose had little effect on Pi10 but affected LAA-950; sharper kernels increased variability. Results argue for standardized protocols for both snapshot and change-over-time COPD imaging.
