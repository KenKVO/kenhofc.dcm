**Abstract.** Small-airway disease in COPD includes mucus plugging, wall remodeling, distortion, and pruning that are poorly represented in generic phantoms. This first-author work builds subject-specific 3D full-generation airway models from patient CT and spirometric data, then uses them in virtual imaging trials with energy-integrating and photon-counting CT. The same modeling pipeline supports the 3D-printed physical lung phantom.

Proc. Virtual Imaging Trials in Medicine 2024;43. Journal manuscript in preparation.
