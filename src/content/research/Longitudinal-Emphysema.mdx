**Abstract.** Longitudinal emphysema CT is confounded when scanners and patients change over time. Clinical data lack ground truth, so algorithms are often judged with clinical surrogates. This study built the first suite of human models with emphysema at multiple time points: 14 virtual subjects across three years. Each model was imaged with DukeSim as an energy-integrating CT scanner at two dose levels and reconstructed with two kernels, slice thicknesses, and pixel sizes. CT-HARMONICA and EmphysemaSeg were tested against ground truth; both reduced longitudinal error (from 6.1±6.3% toward ~1–2% across years 0–5). EmphysemaSeg performed worse when baseline emphysema exceeded 5%, illustrating how VITs can explain algorithm failure modes.
