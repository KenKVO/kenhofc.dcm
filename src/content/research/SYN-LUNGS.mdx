**Abstract.** Screening AI for lung cancer is limited by dataset size, scanner variability, and weak anatomical control in purely generative image models. SYN-LUNGS is a physics- and anatomy-informed framework: public XCAT3 phantoms as digital twins, X-Lesions for nodules with controlled size (4–30 mm), location, and appearance, and DukeSim for vendor-specific CT formation. Evaluated on detection, segmentation, classification, and synthesis, mixed clinical + simulated training improved generalization relative to clinical-only models.
